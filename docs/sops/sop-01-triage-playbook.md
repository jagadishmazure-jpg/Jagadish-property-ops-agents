# Triage playbook

## Severity first
Every incoming work order is triaged before anything else. The triage engine scores urgency, tenant impact, and asset criticality on a 0 to 5 scale and maps the weighted score to P1 through P4. The exact rule that fired is recorded with the decision.

## Safety overrides
Any safety-category report — gas odor, sparking outlets, flooding near electrical panels — is an automatic P1 regardless of the score. Do not wait for a supervisor to confirm a safety P1; confirm the building is safe first, then document.

## Repeat issues
When the same category repeats in the same unit three times within thirty days, bump the priority one level and open a root-cause note. Recurring drain clogs usually mean a line problem, not a tenant problem.

## What good looks like
A triaged ticket names its priority, its score breakdown, and the rule citation. If you cannot cite the rule, re-run triage — never hand-assign a priority from memory.
